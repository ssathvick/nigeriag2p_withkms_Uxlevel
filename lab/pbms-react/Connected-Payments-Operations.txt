1. Programme Admin creates the programme, rules, monthly enrolment and disbursement list.
2. Payment submits the beneficiary list for Finance, Audit and CEO review.
3. Finance, Audit and CEO independently approve the same list revision.
4. Programme Admin configures sponsor bank routing in the approved list's Payments section,
   selecting an existing configured bank route. This is required once per programme.
5. Payment opens that same list, chooses Proceed to payments, reviews the beneficiary count
   and approved total, and chooses Confirm and proceed.
6. View payment progress shows native envelopes, batches and Bridge dispatch/reconciliation
   reports. The same list also remains accessible through the Payments sidebar.

CEO approval alone never sends money. Payment submission is role-checked, logged and
idempotent. Corrections and exclusions from the approved revision are preserved. Payment
records are populated from the signed-off snapshot, without rerunning eligibility or
entitlement rules. Earlier approved lists are not submitted automatically by this update.
The XLS export and approval/evidence history remain available.

Installed Bridge limitation: declared envelope totals are integer quantities. The connected
flow requires whole NGN entitlements and rejects fractional values before approval/payment,
so quantities cannot be silently truncated. Fractional-currency support requires a separate
Bridge database/model migration; this update does not change monetary units or round money.

Backend: nsipa_approval/models.py. Workers: nsipa_payment_snapshot.py and the two
nsipa_disbursement_*_worker.py files, persisted through ConfigMap nsipa-payment-workers.
Generate worker patches with build-payment-workers.py, then ../release/install_payment_workers.py (plan first, then --apply).
The bridge worker runs approved-snapshot and independent-approval checks before batching.
Native addon update: ../release/install_pbms.py (plan first, then --apply). Adapter update: install-adapter.py.
Frontend: npm run build; publish dist via an atomic release symlink. Do not replace the
current Nginx configuration when publishing only static assets.

Validation: evidence/connected-payments/. Synthetic records and local Example Bank only.
